Ludo Plus is the classic race to home for Android. Roll the die, bring your four pawns out of the yard on a 6, race them a full lap of the board, and be the first to get them all home. Land on a rival to send them back to the start.

THE CLASSIC RULES
• Four players: friends passing the phone around, computer opponents, or any mix of the two
• Roll a 6 to leave the yard, and roll again after a 6, a capture, or bringing a pawn home
• Star squares and start squares are safe, and a pawn needs the exact roll to reach home
• Play on for second, third, and last place, or end the game at the first winner

COMPUTER OPPONENTS
• Three levels: Easy plays at random, Medium weighs every move, and Hard thinks a roll ahead

LEARN AS YOU PLAY
• A guided tutorial teaches the game in four short parts: getting out, captures, home, and winning
• The rules, a page an idea, each with a diagram of the board

TRACK YOUR GAMES
• A Leaderboard of the fastest wins
• Statistics for every player: games, wins, streaks, captures, and pawns home
• Leave a game at any time and continue it later

MADE FOR EVERYONE
• Built for TalkBack and large fonts, with every square and move described
• Animation levels, and every animation can be switched off
• No ads, no accounts, and no internet permission: your games stay on your phone
