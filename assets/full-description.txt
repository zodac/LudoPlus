LudoPlus is the classic 'race to home' game, with a few twists of its own! Roll the die, bring your pawns out of the yard on a 6, race them a full lap of the board, and be the first to get them all home. Land on a rival to send them back to the start.

THE CLASSIC RULES
• 2 to 8 players, on a board made for each size
• Friends passing the phone around, computer opponents, or any mix of the two
• Play in teams with 4, 6, or 8 players: two teams, or pairs with partners opposite
• Roll a 6 to leave the yard, and roll again after a 6, a capture, or bringing a pawn home
• Star tiles and start tiles are safe, and a pawn needs the exact roll to reach home
• Play on for every place, or end the game at the first winner

SEVEN WAYS TO PLAY
• Classic: four pawns each, by the classic rules
• Colour Rush: claim the tiles you land on first, take others with a capture, and finish with the most
• Survival: six pawns each and no home; a captured pawn is out, sudden death comes, and the last player with a pawn wins
• Chaos: power-ups on the track, from shields and doubled rolls to swapping places with any pawn
• Haunted House: ghosts drift round the track and send pawns back to the yard
• Portals: land on one and come out at its pair, further on or further back
• Two Dice: roll two dice and move by each in turn; a 6 on either brings a pawn out, and doubles roll again

HOUSE RULES
Mix in the rules your family plays by: Must Capture, Guarded Starts, Free Start, Three Tries, Out on a 1, Blockades, No Capture Roll, No Home Roll, Endless 6s, No Free Pawn, and from one to six pawns each.

PLAY TOGETHER NEARBY
• One game across several phones in the same room, linked directly, with no internet needed
• A player whose phone drops out can rejoin, and the computer plays for them meanwhile

COMPUTER OPPONENTS
• Three levels: Easy plays at random, Medium weighs every move, and Hard thinks a roll ahead

LEARN AS YOU PLAY
• A guided tutorial teaches the game in four short parts: getting out, captures, home, and winning
• The rules, a page an idea, each with a diagram of the board

TRACK YOUR GAMES
• A Leaderboard of the best wins for every mode and number of players
• Statistics for every player: games, wins, streaks, captures, and pawns home
• Leave a game at any time and continue it later

ACHIEVEMENTS AND STYLES
• Over 100 achievements to chase, for captures, comebacks, close finishes, and a few hidden surprises
• Achievements unlock styles: over 200 options of boards, sets of pawns, dice, and backgrounds to choose from

GOOGLE PLAY GAMES
