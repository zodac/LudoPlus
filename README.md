# LudoPlus

<img src="https://github.com/zodac/LudoPlus/releases/download/readme-assets/feature-graphic.gif"
    alt="The LudoPlus logo beside a board mid-game, with pawns drifting behind" width="512">

**The classic race to home, with a few twists of its own.**

LudoPlus is a Ludo game for Android. Roll the die, bring your pawns out of base, race them a full lap of the board,
and be the first to get all four home. Land on a rival to send them back to the start.

> **Get it here.** The APK is on this repo's [Releases page](https://github.com/zodac/LudoPlus/releases/latest); the
> Google Play version is coming.

## What's in it

- **The classic rules** for 2 to 8 players, on a board made for each size, alone or in teams (with 4, 6, or 8
    players), against friends passing the phone around or computer opponents at three levels: Easy, Medium, and Hard
- **Seven game modes**: Classic, Colour Rush, Survival, Chaos, Haunted House, Portals, and Two Dice (see
    [below](#game-modes)), and **eleven house rules** to mix in
- **Play together on phones in the same room** (the Google Play version): one game across several phones, linked
    directly with no server, and a player whose phone drops out can rejoin
- **A guided tutorial** that teaches the game in four short parts, and the rules with a diagram for each idea and a
    board for each mode
- **A Leaderboard** of the best wins for every mode and player count, and **Statistics** for every player: wins,
    streaks, captures, and your total time played
- **Achievements** to chase: over 100 of them, for captures, comebacks, close finishes, and a few hidden surprises
- **Styles** they unlock: 17 boards, 18 sets of pawns, 35 dice, and 20 backgrounds for the table
- Built for **TalkBack** and large fonts, with no accounts: your games stay on your phone
- The Google Play version shows a small ad banner, which an optional purchase removes; this GitHub version has no ads

## Game modes

Every mode has its own Leaderboard, its own page in Statistics, and achievements of its own.

- **Classic**: roll a 6 to bring a pawn out of the yard, race all four a lap of the board and up your home column, and
    land on a rival to send them back. A 6, a capture, and a pawn reaching home each earn another roll; three 6s in a
    row end your turn. Star tiles and start tiles are safe. So nobody is left behind, a player with no pawn out 5
    turns after the first one came out gets one out free, in every mode.
- **Colour Rush**: the first pawn to land on a tile claims it for its colour, and a capture there takes it over.
    Claimed stars are yours for good. The game ends once every tile is claimed or someone gets all their pawns home,
    and the most tiles wins.
- **Survival**: six pawns each, all starting on the board, and no home: pawns lap the board for as long as they last. A
    captured pawn is out of the game, and the last player with a pawn left wins. Hold on too long and Overtime makes
    the start tiles unsafe, Sudden Death the stars, and then the Cataclysm makes tiles vanish from the track until
    there's nowhere left to hide. Its Leaderboard ranks the furthest travelled.
- **Chaos**: power-ups on the track, taken by the pawn that ends its move on one. **Double** makes your next three
    rolls move twice as far, **Shield** bounces a rival off your pawn, **Roll Again** does what it says, **Call Up**
    brings a pawn out of the yard (or saves one for the next pawn captured), and **Swap** trades places with any other
    pawn on the track.
- **Haunted House**: ghosts drift round the track, a die's roll at a time and in either direction, and send any pawn
    they land on back to its yard, unless it's safe.
- **Portals**: pairs of portals on the track, open to every colour. End a move on one and come out at its pair: a
    short hop, or a long jump most of the way round the board, or most of the way back. Then that pair closes and opens
    again somewhere else.
- **Two Dice**: roll two dice and move by each one in turn, with the same pawn or two different ones. A 6 on either
    brings a pawn out, and doubles roll again.

### House rules

Mix any of these into any mode from New Game, once you've finished your first game. A game with house rules doesn't
go on the Leaderboard or in Statistics, and earns only the house rules' own achievements.

- **Pawns**: one to six pawns each, instead of the mode's own number
- **No Capture Roll**: capturing a pawn doesn't earn another roll
- **No Home Roll**: bringing a pawn home doesn't earn another roll
- **Endless 6s**: three 6s in a row don't end your turn
- **Out on a 1**: a 1 brings a pawn out of the yard, as well as a 6
- **Free Start**: while none of your pawns is on the board, any roll brings one out
- **Three Tries**: while none of your pawns is on the board, roll up to three times to bring one out
- **Blockades**: two of your pawns on one tile make a blockade that no other player's pawn can pass or land on
- **Must Capture**: if a move can capture, you have to make a capture
- **Guarded Starts**: a start tile is safe only for its own colour
- **No Free Pawn**: a player with no pawn out 5 turns after the first one came out doesn't get one free

## At the table

- **Tap a player's card** to see their captures and times captured, against each opponent, and how many times they've
    rolled each number.
- **The move log** follows every roll and move, naming pawns by number (it can be turned off in Settings).
- **Your game is saved as you play**: leave at any time and pick it up again with Continue.
- **Make it yours** in Settings: sound effects, vibration, stopping at the first winner, a locked portrait or
    landscape screen, and how much the app animates, down to none at all.
- **Keep your progress** in this GitHub version by exporting it to a file in Settings, and importing it on another
    phone or after a reinstall: your achievements, records, and settings are merged, never overwritten.
