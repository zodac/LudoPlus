# LudoPlus

**The classic race to home, with a few twists of its own.**

LudoPlus is a Ludo game for Android. Roll the die, bring your pawns out of base, race them a full lap of the board,
and be the first to get all four home. Land on a rival to send them back to the start.

> **In development.** LudoPlus isn't released yet: there are no builds on Google Play or this repo's Releases page.

## What's in it

- **The classic rules** for 2 to 8 players, on a board made for each size, alone or in teams, against friends passing
    the phone around or computer opponents at three levels
- **Game modes and house rules**: Quick and Two Dice, nine house rules to mix in, and custom modes of your own
- **Play together on phones in the same room** (the Google Play version): one game across several phones, linked
    directly with no server, and a player whose phone drops out can rejoin
- **A guided tutorial** that teaches the game in four short parts, and the rules with a diagram for each idea
- **A Leaderboard** of the fastest wins, and **Statistics** for every player: wins, streaks, and captures
- **Achievements** to chase: 110 of them, for captures, comebacks, close finishes, and a few hidden surprises
- **Styles** they unlock: 8 boards, 7 sets of pawns, 35 dice, and 20 backgrounds for the table
- Built for **TalkBack** and large fonts, with no accounts: your games stay on your phone
- The Google Play version shows a small ad banner, which an optional purchase removes; this GitHub version has no ads
