# Privacy Policy

[← Back to Documentation](./index.md)

**Last updated:** set when published

This Privacy Policy explains how Ludo Plus ("the App") handles information when you use the App.

## 1. Information We Collect

Ludo Plus is a board game and does not use advertising or analytics services.

The App does not collect or require you to provide personal information such as your name, email address, postal
address, telephone number, or payment information. It has no accounts, no sign-in, and nothing to buy.

The App lets you type in names for the players in a game. These names are stored on your device (see section 2) and are
not sent to us.

The App has no permission to use the internet. Nothing leaves your device through the App, unless you choose to send a
crash report (see section 3).

## 2. Information Stored on Your Device

The App stores on your device:

- Your settings and preferences, and the players and game mode you last set up
- The game in progress, if there is one, so you can continue it
- Your finished games: the player names, places, turns, captures, and times, which make up the App's Leaderboard and
    Statistics

This information is used only to operate the game and provide its features. You can clear it by clearing the App's data
or uninstalling it.

If you have enabled Android's backup feature on your device, Android may include the App's locally stored data in your
device backup (for example, to your Google account). This backup is managed by Android and Google under your device and
account settings, not by the App, and you can turn it off in your device's settings.

## 3. Advertising, Analytics, and Crash Reports

The App does not show advertising, and does not use advertising services or third-party analytics services.

We do not use Google AdMob or similar advertising SDKs.

We do not use analytics services to track how individual users interact with the App.

If the App crashes or stops responding, Google Play may collect technical diagnostic information (such as crash reports)
and share it with us in a form that does not identify you, as described in Google's Privacy Policy
(<https://policies.google.com/privacy>). This depends on your device's settings.

If the App stops because of an error, it shows a page describing the problem, with the option to report it on GitHub. The
App sends nothing by itself. Choosing to report opens your web browser at a new GitHub issue, filled in with:

- The App's version, and how it was installed
- Your device's manufacturer and model, its Android version, and its screen size and font size
- The language in use
- The App's recent actions, such as the pages opened and the moves made, which never include the player names you typed
- The technical details of the error

You can read and change all of it before posting, and nothing is sent unless you post it, which needs a GitHub account of
your own. GitHub issues are public, and GitHub's own terms and privacy statement apply to them. To have a report removed,
contact us (see section 8). You can also copy these details from the page to share them another way.

## 4. Third-Party Services

The App uses no third-party online services. Google Play, from which you may install it, handles its own information
under Google's Privacy Policy and the applicable Google Play terms and policies.

## 5. Children's Privacy

The App is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children, and the App sends nothing about anyone who
plays it.

## 6. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to avoid unnecessary collection of personal information: the App keeps what it stores on your
device, and sends nothing.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time, particularly when new features or third-party services are
introduced. Future versions of the App may add optional features that use Google services, such as signing in to Google
Play Games or an optional purchase through Google Play; this policy will be updated to describe them before any version
that has them is released.

The updated policy will be made available at the same URL as this Privacy Policy, and the "Last updated" date will be
changed accordingly.
